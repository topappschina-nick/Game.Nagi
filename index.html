Windless Isles

A gentle story adventure across seven small islands where the wind has stopped.

A year ago the wind went still and Nagi's grandpa sailed away. She finishes the little kite he left behind, a faint breeze answers, and island by island she sets out to find him: fishing, flying kites, picking tea, lighting snow lanterns and watching the stars with the people she meets.

For Android and iOS. Free to start: the prologue and Lighthouse Cove are free, and one purchase unlocks the other five islands and the finale. Plays offline. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
