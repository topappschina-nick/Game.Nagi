Support

Need help? Check the answers below, or email us. In the game, Settings is the button at the top right of the screen.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase. The same button is also on the card that appears when you set sail for a locked island. Make sure you are online and signed in to the same Google Play account or Apple ID you used to buy. The purchase belongs to your store account, not to us.

I paid, but the islands are still locked.

Try Restore purchase first. If you paid with a method that completes later, the islands unlock once the store confirms the payment. Refunds are handled by the store: Google Play at support.google.com/googleplay, Apple at support.apple.com.

How is my progress saved?

Automatically, on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Your progress stays when you close or update the game, and is removed if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Can I move to a new phone?

Your purchase moves with your store account: on the new phone, sign in with the same Google Play account or Apple ID, install the game and tap Restore purchase. Your progress is saved only on the old device and the game has no online sync. On iPhone and iPad, setting up the new device from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one usually brings it along. On Android, the game is not part of Google’s cloud backup; a direct phone-to-phone transfer during setup may copy it, depending on the phone, but this is not guaranteed.

How do I start over, or go back to an earlier save?

Open Settings. Start over (from the prologue) begins a new game on Windwatch Isle. Restore a save lists earlier copies of your save; tap one to go back to that moment. Before either, the game backs up your current progress (it keeps the five most recent copies), so you can always come back. Your language and your purchase are kept.

How do I change the language?

The game starts in your device’s language when it can. To change it, open Settings and tap Language. There are 10 languages: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français and Русский.

I can’t hear anything.

Open Settings and check that the first button says Sound off (if it says Sound on, tap it). The fishing and kite screens have the same switch. Also check your device’s volume and, on iPhone, Silent mode.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to play. Seeing the price, buying the full game and restoring the purchase need a connection to Google Play or the App Store. Once you own it, the game remembers it on your device, so you can keep playing offline.
