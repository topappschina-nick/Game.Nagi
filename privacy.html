Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This policy describes how Windless Isles (the “game”), published by YIWEN DENG (an individual developer, “we”), handles information. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game and to this website.

Summary

We do not collect, receive or share any personal information. Windless Isles has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and you can play it without an internet connection. The only online activity belongs to the in-app purchase: the game asks Google Play or the Apple App Store for the price and whether you own the full game, buying or restoring it goes through the store, and on Android Google’s purchase library also sends Google its own diagnostic information (see In-app purchase).

What the game stores on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game keeps these files in its private storage on your device:

  • your save: your story progress, the island you are on, the fish you have caught and what your journal holds;
  • up to five earlier copies of your save, made automatically when you start over or restore a save in the game’s settings;
  • pictures of views in the game world, taken by the game for your journal;
  • your settings (the language you chose and whether the Wind Thread is shown) and whether you own the full game;
  • a cache of the game’s own music, so that it starts faster.

These files contain only game data, nothing that identifies you, and the game does not send them anywhere. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

On iOS, your device’s own backup (iCloud or a computer backup) may include these files, according to your device settings. The Android version opts out of Android’s cloud backup of app data; a direct phone-to-phone transfer when you set up a new Android phone may still copy them, depending on the phone. Backups and transfers are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms, and we have no access to them.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports, and we do not sell or share personal data. The game does not contain third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting. The only third-party code in the game that uses the internet is the store’s own purchase library: Google Play Billing Library on Android and Apple’s StoreKit on iOS (see below).

Permissions

The Android version requests vibration, used for light haptic feedback during play, and the Google Play billing permission, used to buy the full game through Google Play. Its Android package also lists internet and network state access: Google’s Play Billing Library adds these for its own communication with Google, described below; the game’s own code does not send anything over the internet. The iOS version does not ask for any permission. The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

In-app purchase

Windless Isles is free to download. The prologue and the first island, Lighthouse Cove, are free to play. A single one-time in-app purchase (“All Islands”) unlocks the other five islands and the finale. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment details, your name or your store account.

To show the price and to know whether you own the purchase, the game asks the store on your device, for example when the game starts, when you buy and when you choose “Restore purchase”. The store answers from your store account, which needs an internet connection; the game talks only to the store, never to a server of ours. If the store cannot be reached, the game keeps its last answer (saved on your device), so you can keep playing offline. We do not run a server and keep no record of purchases.

On Android, purchases use Google’s Play Billing Library, which is built into the game. Google states that the library logs information about how its purchase functions are used (for example whether a request succeeded or failed) and about problems connecting to Google Play, and uses it to improve the library and to support errors. The library sends this to Google itself; we do not receive it. On iOS, purchases use Apple’s StoreKit, and Apple handles all purchase data.

Google’s and Apple’s own privacy policies apply to your payment and purchase data and to the billing library’s diagnostic data.

Children

Windless Isles is a gentle story game suitable for all ages, including children. It has no violence, no frightening content, no advertising, no analytics, no chat or contact with other players, and no links out of the game, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The one purchase is made through Google Play or the App Store, where the store's parental controls (such as purchase approval) apply. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Third parties

You get the game through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which may collect information about your download, your purchases and your use of their store under their own policies (Google, Apple); this includes the purchase processing and the Play Billing Library’s diagnostic data described above. We do not receive personal information from them about you. This website is static: it uses no cookies, analytics, tracking scripts or external fonts. The site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which, like any web host, may process technical request data (such as your IP address) in its server logs under its own policy; we do not have access to that data in a way that identifies you.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct, delete or export. Your saves are on your device and under your control. Data about your purchases and the billing library’s diagnostic data are held by Google or Apple; to exercise your rights over them, use their privacy tools and contacts (linked above). Depending on where you live, you have rights over personal data, including under the EU and UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority), California’s CCPA/CPRA (to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing — we do not sell or share personal information), and Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI). To make any request or ask a question, email us at the address below; if we hold nothing about you, we will tell you so.

We process no personal data of players, so there is no legal basis to state under the GDPR; if you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and delete them when no longer needed. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not transfer it internationally.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game handles data differently (for example by adding online features), we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.
